We evaluated GLM-5.3 with official Harbor rewards on two purposive Terminal-Bench subsets and replayed public trajectories. Initial success was 4/6, 6/6 and 4/6 without defense, with broad sources and with narrowed sources; protected subtrees made no IPv4 connects. The separate network subset achieved 2/3 in every condition. Broad and narrowed policies falsely intervened on 12/51 and 1/67 covered network-task commands. Replay false-call bounds were 2.85–2.92% under broad sources and zero under narrowed sources. Public trust material explains many false labels.
